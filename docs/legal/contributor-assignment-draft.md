# Contributor copyright assignment — draft

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR SIGNING OR USE.**

This document is proposed text for legal review. It is not an executed agreement.
It does not transfer rights merely because it appears in this repository.
Before use, the owner must complete the open fields and adopt a reviewed final version.
The owner must confirm the applicable legal requirements for each rights holder.
Legal terms in this draft retain their legal meanings. No STE conformance claim applies to the agreement text.

**Draft version:** 0.2, October 18, 2026.

Proposed terms now specify Arizona law, Maricopa County, and a private PDF signing process.
Owner adoption and independent legal review remain pending.

## 1. Parties and project

The recipient of the assignment is **Darsh Chaurasia**, referred to as the **Owner**.

The **Contributor** is the individual or legal entity identified in the signature section.
If an entity owns the work, its authorized representative must sign for that entity.

The **Project** is DevWarden, currently hosted at <https://github.com/darshchaurasia/devwarden>.
A change in the Project name or hosting address does not expand the scope of this agreement.

## 2. Contributions covered

A **Contribution** is original code, documentation, tests, designs, or other copyrightable material that the Contributor owns and intentionally submits for incorporation into the Project.

This agreement covers:

- The existing Contributions identified in Schedule A.
- Future Contributions intentionally submitted to the Project after the effective date, to the extent that applicable law permits a prospective assignment.

The agreement does not cover unrelated work, private branches not submitted to the Project, or third-party material that the Contributor does not own.
Material excluded from the assignment must be identified in Schedule B before submission.
A contribution to an unrelated project is not a Contribution under this agreement.

## 3. Copyright assignment

The Contributor hereby assigns to Darsh Chaurasia all of the Contributor's transferable worldwide right, title, and interest in the copyright in the Contributions.
The assignment covers the full period of those rights, including applicable extensions and renewals, subject to rights that applicable law makes nonwaivable or nontransferable.

For future Contributions, the assignment takes effect when the relevant rights arise, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
Where a further instrument is legally necessary, the Contributor agrees to execute that instrument to complete the intended assignment.
Any expenses or additional paid services require a separate written agreement before they are incurred.

The assignment includes the transferable rights to reproduce, modify, distribute, publicly display, publicly perform, license, sublicense, and commercially exploit the Contributions.
The Owner may distribute them under the Project license or other terms, incorporate them into a proprietary product, charge for them, or stop distributing future versions.
The Owner has no obligation to merge, publish, or maintain a Contribution.

No provision transfers another person's rights or removes mandatory statutory rights.
This assignment does not make the Owner the original author of work created by the Contributor.
The Owner must not use this agreement to make a false claim of authorship.

## 4. Contributor authority

The Contributor represents that:

- The Contributor owns the assigned rights and has authority to enter this agreement.
- An entity signatory has authority to bind the rights-holding entity.
- No known employment, university, funding, or other agreement prevents the assignment.
- Contributions do not knowingly include unauthorized third-party material, confidential information, or trade secrets.
- Previously licensed or excluded material is identified in Schedule B, with its source and applicable terms.

If an employer or another entity owns a Contribution, an individual signature alone is not sufficient to transfer that entity's rights.
The actual rights holder must sign the applicable assignment or provide the required authorization.

## 5. Patents and personal rights

This agreement assigns copyright, not ownership of patents.
For patent claims that the Contributor can license and that are necessarily infringed by the Contribution alone or its submitted combination with the Project, the Contributor grants the Owner a worldwide, perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to make, use, sell, import, and distribute that combination, with rights to transfer and sublicense the license.
This grant does not cover unrelated combinations or patent rights that the Contributor cannot license.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the Contributor consents to the Owner's modification, adaptation, and distribution of the Contributions without further approval.
No clause purports to transfer or waive personal or moral rights where applicable law does not permit that result.
The treatment of those rights must be confirmed in legal review before this draft is used.

## 6. Payment, use, and prior licenses

The Contributions are voluntary. No payment or royalty is promised unless separately agreed in writing.
In exchange for the Contributor's assignment and promises, the Owner agrees to give the Contributor a copy of the executed agreement and to keep the contribution and authorship records for the Contributions that the Owner accepts.
The Owner will not remove accurate authorship information from those private records merely because copyright was assigned.
These are proposed mutual promises, not a claim that money has been paid.
Their adequacy and any additional requirements must be confirmed during legal review before adoption.

Unless separately agreed in writing, a Contribution does not create a right to payment, royalties, employment, partnership, or project governance.
This clause does not remove mandatory rights or substitute for legally necessary consideration or execution formalities.

The Contributor may use released Project versions under the applicable Project license or a separate written permission from the Owner.
The assignment does not grant a separate license back to the Contributor.
It does not revoke valid licenses already granted to recipients before the assignment.

## 7. Governing law and administration

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Arizona, United States, without applying its conflict-of-laws rules, and by applicable United States federal law.
This choice does not displace mandatory law that the parties cannot validly exclude.

Subject to applicable subject-matter jurisdiction, the parties consent to exclusive jurisdiction and venue in the state courts located in Maricopa County, Arizona, or the United States District Court for the District of Arizona sitting in Phoenix.
A claim within exclusive federal jurisdiction must be brought in the competent federal court, not a state court.
This clause does not create jurisdiction where the applicable law does not permit it.

This agreement takes effect on the date of the last required signature.
The parties must retain the exact executed version, its schedules, and evidence of authority and signature.
Any amendment must be recorded in writing and accepted by both parties.
Invalidity of a clause does not remove other clauses where the law permits them to operate separately.

The proposed signature method is a private PDF exchange.
Each party may sign the complete PDF by hand or use an electronic signature attached to the complete agreement with the intent to sign it.
The parties consent to exchange the signed agreement and keep the signed records electronically.
The exchange must identify the signatory, agreement version, schedules, signature date, and authority to sign.
Both parties must receive the final executed copy.
A typed name in an unsigned public issue or a pull-request checkbox does not satisfy this agreed process.
Additional formalities required by applicable mandatory law must be satisfied before the Owner accepts the assignment.
